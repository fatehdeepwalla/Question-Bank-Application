THE STORY BEHIND THE PROJECT

Why I Built It

Several online platforms already offer GATE previous-year question banks, allowing users to filter questions by subject and topic (a subtopic within a subject). One such site is practicepaper.in.

The Challenge

While preparing for GATE Computer Science, I found that working through more than 3,000 previous-year questions could take nearly two months.

Effective preparation also requires revisiting questions during revision.

However, repeatedly working through the entire question set can be time-consuming, especially when many questions test similar concepts.

My Approach

I wanted a way to make this revision process more efficient.

I built this application to let me mark questions with tags shown below. This helps me track my progress, identify repetitive questions, prioritize important questions during revision.

I retained the standard filtering features by year, subject, and topic, while adding revision-focused tagging for my own preparation.

  • Solved — track progress
  • Repeated — flag repetitive questions
  • Important — prioritize revision
  • Very Important — highlight key questions
✦

The goal: is to save time and make revision more focused, rather than taking a brute-force appraoch of repeatedly solving the entire question set from scratch.
